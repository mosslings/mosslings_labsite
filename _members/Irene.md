Irene Minz joined the research group in July 2026. She is an alumna of Life Sciences from Sri Venkateswara College and holds a master’s degree in Environmental Studies from the Department of Environmental Studies, both from Delhi University. She is a GATE and UGC-NET JRF fellow, with a broad range of interests including ecology, evolution and conservation.
